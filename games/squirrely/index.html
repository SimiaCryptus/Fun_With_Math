Squirrely

Cross the highway. Fill all four tree hollows. Every car is a driver with a personality — Red hunts, Yellow panics, Blue swerves for you, Green rages in jams, Purple doesn't see you, White is steady.

Arrows/WASD hop · Space dash (2 rows) · hold Shift crouch · T taunt · E drop · Esc pause
Grab acorns off the road (two in your mouth at most) and bank them on the far side. Smoke bombs lie among them — they can't be banked, only dropped (E) to light a roadside distraction. Watch out for motorcycles: faster, and they dodge inside their lane.
Touch: swipe to hop, tap to taunt, double‑tap to dash, long‑press to drop, two‑finger hold to crouch.

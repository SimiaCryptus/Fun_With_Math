The Prime Sieve as a Stack of Orthogonal Periodic Fields

Every prime p emits a period-p exclusion field Mp. Stacked, their combined period is the primorial Lk = p1 · ·· pk, and the genuinely new information carried by pk is the orthogonal kill-set Ck. This lab collects the interactive views, the executable reference generator, and the write-ups in one place.

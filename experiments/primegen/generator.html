Algorithm B — wheeled streaming prime generator — minimal reference for algorithm.md §4

The whole algorithm is 40 lines: a wheel table step[] (§4.2), a min-heap of (value, p, a) records, deferred activation of stream p at n = p² (Lemma 4.2), and the O(1) advancement a += step[a % W]; v = p*a (Thm 4.4). Memory is π(√n) records plus the table — never an array over the range. Nothing is loaded from the network.

Generate §4.3 — unbounded stream, bounded driver

w is the number of wheel primes; W = p₁···p_w, κW = φ(W)/W is the candidate density. Primes ≤ p_w are emitted directly and their composites never become candidates.

Number spiral consume generated primes — square (Ulam) or hexagonal

square: primes ≤ L², side L = ⌊√N⌋ (capped at 8192), laid out on the classic Ulam spiral. hexagonal: primes ≤ 1+3R(R+1) walked around a hex ring spiral (ring k holds 6k cells, consecutive integers are always neighbours), stored in an axial (2R+1)² bitset and drawn by nearest-hex sampling. Zoomed in the exact prime bitset is sampled; zoomed out a pre-computed density mip pyramid is used so no prime is ever dropped by nearest-neighbour downscaling. Twin primes (members of a pair p, p+2) are drawn in red: every mip texel keeps the prime count and the twin count, and is converted to RGB only at the end, so the hue mix stays area-correct at every level. n₀ truncates the spiral at the origin — the centre cell may start counting at 0, 1, 2 or 3.

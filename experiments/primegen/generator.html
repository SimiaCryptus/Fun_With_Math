Algorithm B — wheeled streaming prime generator — minimal reference for algorithm.md §4

The whole algorithm is 40 lines: a wheel table step[] (§4.2), a min-heap of (value, p, a) records, deferred activation of stream p at n = p² (Lemma 4.2), and the O(1) advancement a += step[a % W]; v = p*a (Thm 4.4). Memory is π(√n) records plus the table — never an array over the range. Nothing is loaded from the network.

Generate §4.3 — unbounded stream, bounded driver

w is the number of wheel primes; W = p₁···p_w, κW = φ(W)/W is the candidate density. Primes ≤ p_w are emitted directly and their composites never become candidates.

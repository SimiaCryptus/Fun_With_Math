# Tiny Heroes: A First Adventure Game

_A simplified tabletop adventure for kids ~8 years old_

---

## 1. Overview

**Tiny Heroes** is a beginner-friendly, DnD-style storytelling game.
One player is the **Guide** (the grown-up or older kid who runs the story).
Everyone else plays a **Hero** on a shared adventure.
The Guide can also be an **AI Dungeon Master** (see Section 11). The AI
narrates the story, presents encounters and choices, and tells Heroes
_when_ to roll — but the **Heroes always roll the real foam die** and
tell the AI the number.

- **Players:** 2-5 (1 Guide + 1-4 Heroes)
- **Play time:** 20-40 minutes per adventure
- **Ages:** 7-10
- **You need:** This rulebook, the paper map, character sheets,
  one big foam d20, and pencils.

### Design Goals

- Easy enough to learn in 5 minutes.
- Encourages imagination and teamwork (not competition).
- No reading-heavy rules — pictures and simple words.
- Short play sessions to match kids' attention spans.
- Every roll feels exciting, never punishing.

---

## 2. The Big Foam d20

We use **one shared d20** for everything. Rolling is the fun part!

When the Guide asks for a roll, the Hero rolls the foam die and adds
a small bonus (see character stats). The Guide compares it to a
**Target Number (TN)**:

| Difficulty | Target Number |
| ---------- | ------------- |
| Easy       | 5             |
| Medium     | 10            |
| Hard       | 15            |

- **Roll a 20:** Amazing success! Something extra-cool happens.
- **Roll a 1:** Oops! A funny stumble (never a "you lose" — keep it light).

> **Guide tip:** Lean toward success. If the kid is close, let them
> succeed "with a twist" to keep the story moving.

---

## 3. Character Creation

Creating a Hero takes 4 quick steps. Keep it simple and silly!

### Step 1 — Pick a Hero Type

Each type has one stat that's a little stronger (+2 bonus to those rolls).
All others get +0.

| Hero Type | Strong At | Cool Trick                          |
| --------- | --------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Knight    | Might     | Block an attack to protect a friend |
| Wizard    | Magic     | Cast one "Sparkle Spell" per game   |
| Scout     | Speed     | Move an extra space once per turn   |
| Healer    | Heart     | Heal a friend's hurt once per game  |

### Step 2 — The Four Stats

Every Hero has these four stats. Your Hero Type adds +2 to one of them.

- **Might** — for pushing, lifting, fighting.
- **Magic** — for spells and solving puzzles.
- **Speed** — for running, jumping, sneaking.
- **Heart** — for being brave, kind, and helping.

### Step 3 — Name & Look

Give your Hero a fun name and draw them in the box on the sheet.

### Step 4 — Health Hearts

Every Hero starts with **3 Hearts** ❤️❤️❤️.
Lose a Heart when something bad happens. At 0 Hearts a Hero is
"knocked out" and rests until a friend helps them up (not "dead"!).

---

## 4. The Paper Game Board (Requirements)

The map is a printed/drawn paper board the Heroes move across.

### Layout Requirements

- **Grid:** Large squares (about 1.5 inches / 4 cm each) so the
  foam die and tokens fit nicely.
- **Size:** Fits on a standard table (e.g. A3 or taped A4 sheets).
- **Path:** A clear start (🏠 Home) and end (⭐ Goal).
- **Spaces:** Mix of safe spaces, event spaces (❓), and
  challenge spaces (⚔️ / 🧩).

### Suggested Space Types

| Symbol | Space     | What Happens                   |
| ------ | --------- | ------------------------------ |
| 🏠     | Home      | Starting space                 |
| ❓     | Event     | Guide reads a short event card |
| ⚔️     | Challenge | A monster or obstacle — roll!  |
| 🧩     | Puzzle    | A riddle or task — roll!       |
| 💎     | Treasure  | Find a helpful item            |
| ⭐     | Goal      | Win the adventure!             |

### Art Requirements

- Big, friendly, colorful illustrations.
- Clear icons readable by a young child.
- Optional theme reskins (forest, castle, space).

---

## 5. How to Play (Turn Structure)

Heroes take turns going around the table.

On your turn:

1. **Move** — Move up to **3 spaces** along the path.
2. **Act** — Do one action on your space (explore, fight, help, etc.).
3. **Roll** — If the Guide asks, roll the d20 + your bonus vs. the TN.

After all Heroes go, the Guide describes what the world does next.

### Helping Friends

Any Hero can **Help** another Hero's roll. The helper describes how
they pitch in, and the rolling Hero gets **+2** to their roll.
(Teamwork is encouraged!)

---

## 6. Challenges & Monsters

Monsters are simple and not scary.

- Each monster has a **Target Number** to beat (usually 10).
- On a success, the Hero overcomes the monster.
- On a fail, the Hero loses **1 Heart** and can try again next turn,
  or a friend can Help.

Example monster card:

```
  GIGGLE GOBLIN  ❓
  TN: 10 (Might or Speed)
  "It throws pillows! Dodge or knock it over."
```

---

## 7. Winning the Game

The Heroes **win together** by reaching the ⭐ Goal space and
completing the final challenge. There are no losers — if Heroes
get knocked out, friends help them back up.

## 11. The AI Guide (Dungeon Master)

The AI plays the **Guide**. It runs the story for the Heroes but **never
rolls the dice itself** — rolling is the kids' favorite part!

### Golden Rules for the AI Guide

1. **Never roll for the players.** When a roll is needed, say _who_ rolls,
   _which stat_ to use, and the **Target Number**, then **ask for the
   number** and wait.
   > Example: "Pip the Scout, roll the foam die for **Speed**!
   > You need a **10**. What did you get?"
2. **Wait for the kid's answer**, then add their bonus and compare to the TN.
3. **Keep it short and silly.** A few friendly sentences at a time, simple
   words, lots of sound effects (_BONK!_, _whoosh!_).
4. **Lean toward success** (see Section 2 Guide tip). Close rolls succeed
   "with a twist." A natural **20** is amazing; a **1** is a funny stumble,
   never "you lose."
5. **No scary content.** Monsters are goofy, not frightening (pillow-throwing
   goblins, grumpy mud puddles, ticklish dragons).
6. **Encourage teamwork.** Remind Heroes they can **Help** (+2) each other.

### What the AI Guide Presents

- **Encounters / Conflicts** — a goofy monster or obstacle with a TN and the
  stat(s) that can beat it (see Section 6).
- **Choices** — offer 2-3 clear options and let the Heroes pick.
  > "Do you (A) sneak past the snoring troll, (B) tickle it awake, or
  > (C) build a pillow bridge?"
- **Events** — short surprises from the ❓ spaces.
- **A Basic Map** — a simple text/ASCII path so kids can see where they are.

### Drawing the Basic Map (text version)

When no paper board is handy, the AI draws a simple line of spaces using the
Section 4 symbols, and marks each Hero's position. Keep it short (about
8-12 spaces from 🏠 to ⭐).

```
   🏠 -- ❓ -- ⚔️ -- 💎 -- 🧩 -- ❓ -- ⚔️ -- ⭐
   ^Pip
```

- Use the legend from Section 4 (🏠 Home, ❓ Event, ⚔️ Challenge,
  🧩 Puzzle, 💎 Treasure, ⭐ Goal).
- Show each Hero token under their current space (e.g. `^Pip`, `^Mira`).
- Redraw the map whenever Heroes move so they always know where they are.

### Turn Flow with the AI Guide

1. The AI shows the **map** and whose turn it is.
2. The Hero says how far they **Move** (up to 3 spaces).
3. The AI describes the space and any **encounter or choice**.
4. If a roll is needed, the AI states **who / stat / Target Number**, then
   **asks for the rolled number**.
5. The Hero rolls the real die and tells the AI the value.
6. The AI adds the bonus, compares to the TN, and narrates the (fun!) result.
7. Repeat for each Hero, then describe what the world does next.

### Quick Start the AI Can Say

> "Welcome, Tiny Heroes! First, tell me each Hero's **name**, **type**
> (Knight, Wizard, Scout, or Healer), and we'll begin at 🏠 Home. Ready?"
